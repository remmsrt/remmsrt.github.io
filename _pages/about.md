I am a PhD student at [CERMICS](https://cermics-lab.enpc.fr) and [BNP Paribas CIB](https://cib.bnpparibas), advised by [Julien Guyon](https://cermics.enpc.fr/~guyon//), [Grégoire Loeper](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gregoire-loeper-7252bb6/) and [Loucas Pillaud-Vivien](https://thebiglouloup.github.io/loucaspillaudvivien/). 

Previously, I graduated with a Master’s degree in Quantitative Finance ([M2MO](https://masterfinance.math.univ-paris-diderot.fr) <img src="{{ '/assets/img/M2MO_logo.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="M2MO logo" style="height:2em; vertical-align:middle; margin-left:0.25em; margin-top:-0.2em;">) from ENSAE Paris and Université Paris Cité. I completed my Master’s thesis during a research internship at Capital Fund Management ([CFM](https://www.cfm.com) <img src="{{ '/assets/img/CFM_logo.png' | relative_url }}" alt="CFM logo" style="height:0.9em; vertical-align:middle; margin-left:0.25em; margin-top:-0.2em;">). Then, I did a research internship at [OIST](https://www.oist.jp), Okinawa, Japan, before starting my PhD.

Prior to that, I obtained a Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics and Data Science from Université Paris Cité, and a Bachelor's degree in applied mathematics and computer science from Université Gustave Eiffel.
