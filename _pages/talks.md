## 2025

### Flow-based generative models: how to train them efficiently using flow matching and optimal transport  
📍 [OIST MLDS Unit Seminar](https://groups.oist.jp/mlds/event/seminar-mlds-unit-seminar-2025-4-mr-r%C3%A9mi-surat-ensae-paris-ms-klea-ziu-mbzuai)

**Abstract**  
This talk aims to present generative models based on flows, which are currently state-of-the-art methods in generative AI for continuous spaces.  
We begin with a brief overview of energy-based models, one of the earliest approaches to model probability distributions, which provide the basic ideas for understanding the difficulties of data generation.  
We then focus on flow-based models and their efficient training through flow matching, while also explaining their connections to the well-known diffusion models.  
Particular emphasis is placed on how optimal transport can serve as a framework for defining dynamic flows in probability space.  
Finally, we discuss further links with Schrödinger bridges, highlighting the unifying perspective they bring to modern generative modeling.
